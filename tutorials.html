Slip Knot (a Few Ways!)

The slip knot is where almost every crochet project begins. Other than the magic ring, it's basically the foundation of everything! There are so many ways to make one, and everyone has the one that works for them! The first video is closest to how I make mine, and the second shows a few more methods. Try them out and see which one clicks. Credits to the owners of the videos!

The Basics

Single Crochet (sc)

One of the first stitches most people learn! Once you get the hang of it, a lot of other stitches work the same way, just with a different number of loops on your hook. Credits to the owner of the video!

The Basics

Magic Ring (Magic Circle)

Beginners usually get intimidated by this one (I did too!), but it's really not too bad! You can use this in many projects, it's so so helpful. Credits to the owner of the video!

The Basics

Double Crochet (dc)

The taller cousin of the single crochet! It works a lot like sc, just with an extra yarn over at the start, so your rows grow way faster. Credits to the owner of the video!

Mini Dinosaur

One of my favorite patterns! Best of all, no sewing involved! Credits to the owner :)

R1: Magic ring, 8sc, then pull to tighten [8]
R2: inc*8 [16]
R3: (sc,inc)*8 [24]
R4: (2sc, inc)*8 [32]
R5: (7sc, bobble) *4 [32]
R6-R9: 32sc [32]
R10: 7sc, 6dec, 13sc [26]
R11: 4sc, 6dec, 10sc [20]
R12: 2sc, 6dec, 6sc [14]
R13: 2sc, 2 dec, 8sc [12]
R14-R16: 12sc [12]
R17: 10sc, 2nc [14]
R18: 10sc, 4 inc [18]
R19: 13sc, 2inc, 3sc [20]
R20-R21: 20sc [20]
R22: (3sc, dec)*4 [16]
Insert eyes by this point, if using safety eyes
R23: dec*8 [8]
You can add some dec to make it smaller if you want (optional!) before cutting the yarn and weaving it in to close the top

Mini Loaf Chicken

A cute and simple pattern! They remind me of those Stardew Valley chickens haha. Credits to the owner!!

R1: 8sc in mr, slst into the first sc (8)
R2: 8inc, slst to the first st (16)
R3: (1inc, 1sc) *8 (24)
R4-R8: 24sc (24)
R9: fold bowl in half, slst into the first stitches, (ch2, sc1 in the next pair of stitches)*2, 2ch, slst, stuff the chicken, 5slst, fasten off
Attach the red yarn, (3ch, 1sc in the next pair of stitches)*2, 1sc (last one a bit tighter than the rest), fasten off (sew the ends in)
Embroider the beak, use your preferred method of attaching eyes
