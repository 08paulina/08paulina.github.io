hi, i'm the hook behind hooked

It all started with a scarf my aunt crocheted for me. One look at it and I thought: wait, could I make one of these? A lot of practice later, the answer is yes, and my room is now home to a small army of dinosaurs, chickens, and a few creatures that remain unidentified.

Then I started bringing projects to school, and people kept asking what I was making. My answer was always some version of "want me to show you?" A few hooks and a lot of tangled yarn later, my friends were crocheting too, and Hooked was born: a nonprofit where we turn all those stitches into stuffed animals for kids in need. (And honestly? Getting to hang out together while we do it is half the fun.)

The video is a little peek at what that actually looks like. It's sped up, because real-time crocheting a dinosaur is a lot of sitting very still for a very long time.
